# Original surface generation prompts

Both images were generated for TARUKH using the built-in image-generation tool. They were encoded as local WebP assets and load only from the game’s own host during setup. No image-generation service runs during play.

## Mars regolith

Output: `public/art/mars-regolith-original.webp`, 768×768.

Create ONE original seamless tileable PBR-style albedo texture for the ground in a realistic Mars exploration videogame. A square 1024x1024 texture viewed perfectly straight down, orthographic and evenly lit, of dry fine rusty ochre sand mixed with fine grey-brown basalt grit, tiny angular stones 2 to 15 mm across, subtle eroded wind comb ripples, powdery red-orange iron oxide dust. Rich natural photographic material detail like a high quality AAA game texture. Overall warm muted burnt sienna and ochre, never bright red or vivid orange. Absolutely no horizon, no scene, no buildings, no objects, no astronaut, no typography, no frame, no labels. No baked shadows, no dramatic directional light, no perspective. Same detail scale and color from edge to edge, perfectly seamless-looking, no central focal point, no big rock formations. This is the raw flat base-color surface material itself, not a rendering of a sample sphere.

## Ivory pressure panel

Output: `public/art/mars-ivory-panel-original.webp`, 512×512.

One original photorealistic game material texture, perfectly orthographic straight-on flat square image. Off-white warm ivory powder-coated aerospace metal pressure panel. Extremely fine realistic paint grain, subtle rubbed edge wear, faint ochre Mars dust accumulation at the lower edge, a very fine recessed vertical seam near each side edge, and four tiny metal fastener heads near the corners. Neutral flat diffuse lighting with no scene shadows. Most of the panel is gently worn clean ceramic ivory metal, no writing, no text, no logos, no graphics, no frame, no objects, no perspective, no 3D sample sphere. High quality believable realistic material for a Mars habitat, understated grunge, never distressed or rusty all over. Seamless-looking neutral ivory edges. Square 1024x1024.
